## 7. 첫 댓글 (Maker's comment) — **형 목소리 초안, 형이 고쳐 쓸 것**
```
Hi Product Hunt 👋

I build structures for a living — schools, retrofits, safety inspections.
Which means I spend a stupid amount of time fighting PDFs at 2 a.m.:
a tender portal that only accepts 300 KB, a scanned contract nobody can search,
a merge that has to happen before the deadline in nine minutes.

Every tool I found wanted an account, stamped a watermark on my file,
or asked me to upload a client's drawings to someone else's server.
So we built the one we wanted.

PDF300 has 24 tools. 14 run fully in your browser — the file never leaves your
machine. The heavy ones (OCR, password, repair, Office→PDF) run on our server.
No sign-up, no watermark, nothing to install.

It's free and unlimited while we're in beta. If it saves you one late night,
that's the whole point.

Happy to answer anything — and I'd genuinely like to know which tool you
reach for first.
```
