What fits in 300 KB

With 300 KB a single page can reach about 150 dpi — sharp enough to print. Three pages land around 88 dpi in our tests, and ten pages around 49 dpi. If you need a detailed walk-through of the method, read how to reduce a PDF to under 300 KB without making it unreadable.

Where you will see a 300 KB limit

  • Campus France India. For the CEF (Études en France) process, the FAQ says “each document should not exceed 300KB” (Campus France India FAQ).
  • Some exam and job portals use 300 KB for identity proofs and certificates. Figures quoted on coaching sites vary, so use the number printed in your own notice.

If it won’t go below 300 KB, try this

  1. Scan both sides onto one page.If the form wants the front and back of an ID, put both on one A4 page. One page at 300 KB is crisp.
  2. Remove the cover letter.Portals usually want the document, not a covering letter. Fewer pages means more detail on the ones that matter.
  3. Try server mode for text PDFs.If your PDF is mostly typed text with one heavy image, high-quality compression keeps the text as text and may reach 300 KB without blurring anything.
  4. Check the result at 100%% zoom.Read the smallest number on the page — a passport number or date of birth — before uploading.

Questions

What resolution will my pages get at 300 KB?

In our tests: one page about 150 dpi, two pages about 128 dpi, three pages about 88 dpi, ten pages about 49 dpi.

Why does my 2-page PDF from my bank already fit?

Statements generated by software are stored as text, not images, so they are usually small already. The tool tells you if the file is already under the target.

Will the PDF still open on the portal?

Yes. The output is a standard PDF with one JPG image per page, which every viewer and upload system accepts.
