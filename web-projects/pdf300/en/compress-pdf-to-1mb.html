What fits in 1 MB

1 MB is roughly 1,000 KB to spread over the pages. In our tests three colour pages kept 169 dpi, six pages about 98 dpi and ten pages about 103 dpi at JPG quality 50. India's customs procedure gives a matching rule of thumb: at 75 KB per black-and-white page, “the maximum permissible file-size is 1 MB … would allow the submitter to upload a supporting document which is up to 13 pages long” (e-SANCHIT procedure).

Where you will see a 1 MB limit

  • Passport Seva at Indian Embassies and Consulates. The applicant-portal SOP says: maximum size allowed 1 MB, the total of all uploaded documents not more than 1 MB, PDF format (SOP for document uploading, Embassy of India, Brussels). Note the total: three documents must share 1 MB.
  • ICEGATE e-SANCHIT (Indian customs). Maximum 1 MB per file, scanned at not less than 200 dpi in black and white, about 75 KB per A4 page (step-by-step procedure).

If it won’t go below 1 MB, try this

  1. Watch for total limits.When a portal caps the total (like Passport Seva abroad), divide: three documents means about 330 KB each. Use the target-size tool at 330 KB.
  2. Split long files.e-SANCHIT says to split documents over 1 MB into two or more files. Split PDF by page range.
  3. Text PDFs with big images.Try high-quality compression first; it keeps text selectable.
  4. For email, aim lower.1 MB attaches anywhere, but a phone on a weak connection will thank you for 500 KB.

Questions

Is 1 MB here 1,000 KB or 1,024 KB?

We aim for under 1,000,000 bytes, so the file passes portals that count either way.

How many pages fit in 1 MB?

About 13 black-and-white pages at the e-SANCHIT standard, or 6–10 mixed scans at around 100 dpi in our tests.

My file is 1.2 MB of text. Should I use this?

Probably not — rasterising text makes it bigger per page. Use high-quality compression, which keeps the text.

Does anything get uploaded?

Not in browser mode, which is the default. Server mode sends the file to our server, processes it in memory and does not keep it.
