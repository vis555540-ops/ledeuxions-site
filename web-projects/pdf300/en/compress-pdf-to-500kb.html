What fits in 500 KB

At 500 KB, three pages each get about 160 KB, which bought 109 dpi in our test — clear on screen. Six pages still reached about 96 dpi, and ten pages about 72 dpi, where normal-size text remains readable. Single pages can stay at 200 dpi, the highest setting this tool uses.

Where you will see a 500 KB limit

  • Indian e-Visa. The document upload page sets PDF files to a minimum of 10 KB and a maximum of 500 KB (indianvisaonline.gov.in — Document Upload).
  • Recruitment portals. For example, the Sardar Patel University of Police, Security and Criminal Justice (Rajasthan) 2022 recruitment required A4 PDF documents “not exceeding 500 KB” (SPUP document upload guidelines).

If it won’t go below 500 KB, try this

  1. Mind the minimum too.The e-Visa page also has a 10 KB minimum. Our output never comes near that, but a blank page might.
  2. Send passport pages as one PDF.Join the photo page and address page with Merge PDF, then compress once — two pages at 500 KB look much better than two separate 250 KB files.
  3. Straighten crooked scans first.A tilted page has more edges to store. Rotate PDF fixes 90° turns; for small tilts, rescan.
  4. Ten pages or more?Use Black & white, or split the file if the portal allows several uploads.

Questions

Is 500 KB enough for a 10-page document?

For typed or printed text, yes — our 10-page test landed at 72 dpi, still readable at normal sizes. Handwriting and small print will be soft.

Does the e-Visa portal accept image-only PDFs?

The upload page lists only the format and size limits. An image-based PDF is a normal PDF, so it meets both.

Can I compress several files at once?

Not yet. Merge them first if the portal wants one file, or compress them one by one.

Why does the result sometimes land well under 500 KB?

If the file fits at the highest setting (200 dpi, quality 80–88), there is no point in making it bigger.
